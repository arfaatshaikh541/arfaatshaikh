# Milestone 11 Unit 4: Partner Governance and Integration Certification

This unit closes Milestone 11 with governed partner onboarding, publishable integration listings, independent security reviews, bounded certifications, and incident-driven suspension controls.

## Safety boundaries

- Partners require an active organisation, public HTTPS website, privacy contact, and accepted terms.
- Listings require a verified partner, active application, allowlisted scopes, a passed security review, and active certification.
- `assistant.request` cannot be marketplace-published through the ordinary listing path.
- Security reviews require independent review, data minimisation, deletion verification, and zero open critical or high findings.
- Certifications expire within 365 days and require evidence fingerprints.
- Open high-severity incidents require credential revocation; unresolved critical incidents require listing suspension.

## Deployment limitations

Live marketplace publication, external partner due diligence, real penetration testing, credential revocation propagation, and incident paging remain deployment work.
