# Research Workspace Foundation

Milestone 8 Unit 1 adds private-by-default research workspaces, role-scoped collaboration, collections, reading lists, annotations, and provenance-preserving citations.

## Trust boundaries
- Canonical Islamic claims continue to come from governed source passages.
- Research annotations are user-authored notes and can never become Islamic evidence.
- External references require HTTPS and remain visibly distinct from canonical sources.
- Workspace access follows owner, editor, contributor, and viewer capabilities.
- Citation records retain rendered text and a provenance payload for reproducibility.

## Deferred
Persistence CRUD, export formats, collaborative revision history, moderation, scholar review, and knowledge-network visualisation belong to later Milestone 8 units.
