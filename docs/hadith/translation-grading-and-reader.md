# Hadith translation, grading, and reader governance

Hadith translations are stored separately from the Arabic matn and require an approved Source Registry edition, exact passage provenance, deterministic manifest reconciliation, human approval, and final source revalidation before publication.

Grading imports preserve every attributed scholarly opinion as a distinct record. The platform never derives a synthetic consensus or replaces the grader's wording with its own judgement. A normalized label supports filtering, while `grading_text`, `grader_name`, methodology notes, and passage provenance preserve the actual opinion and its source.

Public chapter reading endpoints expose only published collections, hierarchy records, narrations, translations, and gradings. Stable fragments use `#hadith-{collection_hadith_number}`. The bilingual reader renders Arabic as primary text, labels translations, shows grading opinions separately, and copies canonical references with deep links.

No hadith, translation, or grading content is bundled by this implementation.
