# Milestone 19 Architecture: Trusted Islamic Life Infrastructure

Milestone 19 adds deterministic governance contracts and durable records for prayer and Hijri timekeeping, halal standards and traceability, ethical commerce, Shariah-governed financial products, safeguarded family services, Islamic heritage stewardship, and final acceptance.

These controls are decision-support and audit infrastructure. They do not replace competent religious authorities, regulators, courts, licensed certifiers, financial institutions, safeguarding professionals, or heritage agencies.
