# Milestone 15: Living Islamic Civilization Platform

Milestone 15 adds governed scholarly councils, deterministic provenance lineage, cross-institution research controls, curriculum and certification governance, evidence-bound community contributions, stewardship handovers, privacy-safe analytics, API ecosystem maturity, and an explicit portable-versus-production acceptance gate.

Sensitive Islamic content remains evidence-bound and requires scholarly approval. Minority and dissenting views are preserved in decision records rather than erased. Analytics releases require privacy controls, published metric definitions, and bias review.
