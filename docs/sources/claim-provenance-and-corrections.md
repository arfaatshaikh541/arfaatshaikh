# Claim provenance, corrections, and supersession

Milestone 2 Unit 3 introduces atomic source claims as editorial records. These are not AI answers and do not assert religious authority by themselves.

A claim may cite one or more immutable source passages. Each link records whether the passage supports, qualifies, disputes, or contextualises the claim, plus the exact character span and an editorial rationale.

Corrections never rewrite an already cited passage. A correction request is decided explicitly and an accepted request points to a newly versioned replacement passage. Edition supersession is restricted to another edition of the same source, retires the old edition, and immediately disables its retrieval eligibility.

Approval policies are versioned by source type. Activating a policy retires the former active policy, preserving the historical governance rules under which earlier decisions were made.
