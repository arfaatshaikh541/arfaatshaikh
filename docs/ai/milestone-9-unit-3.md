# Milestone 9 Unit 3: Personalization and Accessibility Governance

Personalization is opt-in, explainable, and restricted to explicit educational preferences, language availability, bookmarks, dismissals, and course progress. The platform must not infer piety, sect, madhhab, morality, ethnicity, politics, health, disability, spiritual rank, or religious worth.

Accessibility settings alter presentation only. They are never used to rank users, infer capability, or decide religious content.

Recommendations fail closed when prerequisites or preferred-language content are unavailable, and users can disable personalization or dismiss candidates at any time. Events carry a consent basis and bounded retention period.
