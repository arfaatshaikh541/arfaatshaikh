1. Governed retrieval projection and revocation-aware evidence contracts.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

The assistant cannot publish unsupported claims, execute instructions found inside retrieved text, fabricate consensus, or convert private notes and feedback into Islamic evidence. High-risk case-specific rulings escalate to a qualified scholar.
