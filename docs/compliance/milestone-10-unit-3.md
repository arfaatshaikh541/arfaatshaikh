# Milestone 10 Unit 3: Compliance and Enterprise Readiness

Implements governed framework/control records, immutable evidence fingerprints and retention, enterprise risk scoring, disaster-recovery objectives, and a fail-closed enterprise release gate.

Supported framework identifiers: ISO27001, SOC2, GDPR, UAE_PDPL, and NIST_CSF. Inclusion is a control-mapping capability, not a certification claim.

Production readiness requires verified controls and evidence, resolved high risks, tested encrypted restore and rollback, verified key/secret rotation, and tested tenant data export and deletion. Live certification, legal review, and real recovery exercises remain deployment obligations.
